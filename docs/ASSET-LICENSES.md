# Asset and License Record

All active illustrations are original canvas drawing code in `src/artwork.ts`. The colorful stick figures use round smiling heads, colored pencil limbs, simple poses, and small domain props. The five figure colors correspond to Tech, Research, Management, Design, and CC. Group drawings appear on the final pages and back cover.

The user supplied cartoon crops and the earlier AI generated atlas have been removed from the website. No external character images are loaded or bundled.

Front and back hardcovers use a procedural cloth weave and gold colored lettering. Paper grain, desk grain, comic borders, speech bubbles, and application worksheets are also generated locally. Fonts use system stacks. Three.js, TypeScript, and Vite are existing dependencies; consult their package metadata for their licenses. Qbject remains an interaction reference; none of its image or video assets are bundled.
